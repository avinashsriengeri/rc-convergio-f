## 🎨 Usage Guidelines

### **Primary Actions**
- Use **Blue (#2563EB)** for primary buttons and CTAs
- Use **Blue gradients** for important headers and highlights

### **Status Indicators**
- **Green (#10B981)** for success, completed, positive states
- **Orange (#F59E0B)** for warnings, in-progress, pending states  
- **Red (#EF4444)** for errors, failed, negative states
- **Gray (#9CA3AF)** for disabled, inactive, neutral states

### **Journey Stages**
- **Blue** for Lead Generation (beginning)
- **Green** for Email Campaign (engagement)
- **Purple** for Deal Proposal (sales)
- **Orange** for Company Creation (setup)
- **Red** for Deal Closure (completion)

### **Backgrounds**
- **White (#FFFFFF)** for cards and content areas
- **Gray-50 (#F9FAFB)** for page backgrounds
- **Gradient (#F8F9FF to #FFFFFF)** for main app background
